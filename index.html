FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Got Questions?

Here are answers to the most common questions clients ask before starting.

How do I send my raw video files to you?

You can upload your raw files to Google Drive, Dropbox, WeTransfer, or Frame.io and share the folder link with me. I will handle the download securely.

What is your typical turnaround time for a video?

For short-form Reels & Shorts, turnaround is usually 24 to 48 hours. For standard 10-15 minute YouTube videos, delivery takes 2 to 4 business days depending on complexity.

Do you offer revisions if I want changes made?

Yes! I provide unlimited minor revisions until you are 100% satisfied with the final video cut and design.

Can I hire you on a monthly retainer basis?

Absolutely! I offer discounted monthly retainer packages for creators and businesses needing a consistent volume of weekly videos.
